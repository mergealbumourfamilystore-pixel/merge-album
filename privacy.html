PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ikra ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Merge Album: Our Family Story (the "App"). By downloading, installing, or playing the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The App is designed to collect as little information as possible.

  a) Locally stored data
     Your game progress (levels completed, in-game currency, settings such as
     sound and vibration preferences) is stored only on your own device. We
     do not currently operate any server that receives, stores, or has
     access to this information.

  b) Information we do NOT currently collect
     The App does not currently require account creation or login, and does
     not currently collect your name, email address, or other personal
     contact information.

  c) Advertising (if enabled)
     The App may display advertisements served through Google AdMob. When
     ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may automatically
     collect certain technical information (such as your device's
     advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information) to
     serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:
     https://policies.google.com/privacy
     If and when this feature is active, you may be able to limit
     personalized advertising through your device's settings (e.g. "Opt out
     of Ads Personalization" on Android).

  d) Permissions
     The App may request the device Vibration permission, which is used
     only to provide haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. when tapping a
     button). This permission does not give the App access to any personal
     information.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Locally stored game data is used solely to let you resume your progress
between play sessions on the same device. Any advertising data described in
section 1(c) is used and processed by Google/AdMob to display and measure
advertisements, not by us directly.

3. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except for the
advertising service described above (Google AdMob), which processes limited
technical data under its own privacy policy.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is intended for a general, family audience and is not directed
specifically at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address
below so we can address it.

5. DATA SECURITY

Because your game data is stored locally on your device, its security also
depends on the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your
device's operating system up to date and using a passcode or biometric
lock.

6. YOUR CHOICES

  - You can reset your local game progress from within the App's settings
    (where available), which deletes the locally stored save data.
  - You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored
    data associated with it.
  - Where advertising is active, you can adjust ad personalization settings
    through your device's system settings.

7. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER THE GDPR (EU/EEA USERS)

The data controller for the App is Ikra, Germany
(mergealbumourfamilystore@gmail.com). Advertising data is processed by
Google AdMob on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which is
requested through a consent form the first time you open the App and can
be changed at any time. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing,
to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have
the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory
authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we
add new features such as accounts, cloud save, or additional third-party
services. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the
most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes take effect
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
