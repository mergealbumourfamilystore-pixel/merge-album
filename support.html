HELP & SUPPORT

Need a hand with Merge Album: Our Family Story? Here's how to get help.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: I lost my progress after reinstalling the app / switching phones.
A: Right now, your progress is saved only on the device you're playing on.
   If you uninstall the App or switch to a new phone, that local save is
   lost. (We're working on account sign-in for cross-device saves in a
   future update.)

Q: I ran out of lives (hearts). What do I do?
A: Lives refill automatically over time, or you can watch a short video to
   get one instantly. Wait for the next heart, or come back a bit later!

Q: The game froze or crashed.
A: Try closing the App completely and reopening it. If it keeps happening,
   please contact us below with your device model and Android version so
   we can look into it.

Q: How do I turn off sound, music, or vibration?
A: Open Settings from the main map screen — you can toggle Sound Effects,
   Music, and Vibration there.

Q: How do I contact you about something else?
A: Email us at mergealbumourfamilystore@gmail.com — please include your device model and a
   short description of the issue (and a screenshot, if you can) so we can
   help as quickly as possible.
